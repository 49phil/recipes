# Ingredients

* for 3 (5x6cm) x2 form
* 5ml hot water
* 72.5g sugar
  * 32.5g for caramel
  * 40g for pudding
* 3 eggs
* 250g milk
* 1tsp vanilla extract

# Recipe

1. add sugar into a pot
2. heat until caramelized and dark brown, then add a bit of hot water and mix together
3. pour into the forms
4. whisk 3 eggs together and sieve them
5. add sugar and milk
6. whisk until sugar has dissolved
7. add vanilla
8. refrigerate for 2 hours
9. cover the forms with aluminum foil and punch a couple of holes around the rim
10. put them on a baking sheet, add around 1cm of 40C water around
11. bake at 140C for 60 to 70 minutes
12. refrigerate for 4 hours
