# Ingredients

* 100g couverture chocolate 56%
* 45g butter
* 45g heavy cream
* 3 eggs
* 90g sugar
  * 40g for egg yolks
  * 50g for egg whites
* 20g flour
* 40g cocoa powder
* optional: powdered sugar as garnish

# Recipe

1. melt chocolate and butter over a double boiler
2. separate egg whites and yolks
3. keep egg white in the refrigerator until needed
4. add 40g of sugar to egg yolk and whisk together
5. add heavy cream to melted choclate and whisk together
6. add chocolate mixture to egg yolk and whisk together
7. add 50g of sugar to refrigerated egg whites
8. whip until soft peaks form
9. add 1/3 of the meringue to the chocolate mixture and mix
10. sift in flour and cocoa powder
11. mix well by folding it in
12. add remanining meringue and fold it in
13. pour batter into a pan lined with parchment paper
14. bake in a preheated oven at 160C for 43 to 45 minutes or once a skewer comes out clean
15. let it cool and then keep it in the fridge until serving
16. garnish with powdered sugar
