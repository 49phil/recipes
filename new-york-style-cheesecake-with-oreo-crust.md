# Ingredients

* 200g cream cheese
* 50g sugar
* 100g sour cream
* 2 eggs (~100g)
* 10g flour
* 40g white chocolate
* 65g milk
  * 15g for crust
  * 50g for cheese cake
* 8 oreo cookies
* 15cm cake pan

# Recipe

1. bring cream cheese to room temperature
2. crush oreos finely in a bag
3. add them to a bowl and add 15g of milk
4. mix together
5. press it in the bottom of your cake pan
6. put the cake pan in the refrigerator for later
7. preheat oven to 150C
8. add the chocolate and 50g of milk to a bowl
9. melt it, either in a microwave or over a double boiler
10. add cream cheese to a bowl
11. add sugar and whisk together
12. add sour cream and whisk it in
13. whisk the eggs in a different bowl
14. add the eggs one half at a time to the cream cheese and whisk it in
15. sift flour and whisk it in
16. add the chocolate to the cream cheese
17. sift the batter as well and add it to the cake pan
18. bake in a water bath (about 2cm) for 45 to 55 minutes
19. once it has a light golden brown color and the cake has baken through (you can test this by shaking it), take it out of the oven and let it cool down
20. after it has cooled down, cover it in plastic wrap and let it chill in the fridge
