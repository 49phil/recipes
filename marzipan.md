# Ingredients

* 200g blanched almonds
* 100g powdered sugar
* 1tsp rose water
* 5 drops bitter almond oil
* water as needed

# Recipe

1. add almonds to a food processor
2. grind them finely but without any oil leaking out
3. sieve powdered sugar in a separate bowl
4. add ground almonds
5. add rose water
6. add bitter almond oil
7. combine it, if it is to coarse or doesn't come together, add a bit of water but only conservatively
