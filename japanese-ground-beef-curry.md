# Ingredients

* 1lb (~450g) ground beef
* 2 cloves of garlic
* 1 pinch of salt
* 1 pinch black pepper
* 1/2 onion
* 1 japanese curry cube
* 1tsp dashi powder
* 1tsp garlic powder
* 1/2 cup milk
* 1tbsp ketchup
* optionally: 2tbsp bonito flakes as a topping

# Recipe

1. dice the onion
2. mince the garlic
3. add vegetable oil to a pan and saute the garlic
4. add ground beef and cook until golden brown
5. season with salt and black pepper
6. set it aside
7. use the same, now empty pan, for the diced onion
8. cook the onion for 2 minutes or until translucent
9. add the curry cube over low heat
10. once it is melted, add the beef back in
11. season with dashi and garlic powder
12. add milk
13. add ketchup
14. serve with rice and optionally top with bonito flakes
