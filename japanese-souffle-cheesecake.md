# Ingredients

* 200g cream cheese
* 20g butter
* 130g milk
* 40g egg yolk
* 110g egg white
* 10g flour
* 55g sugar
  * 15g for egg yolk
  * 40g for egg white

# Recipe

1. add cream cheese to a bowl, quickly whisking it and set it aside
2. add egg yolks to a bowl
3. add 15g of sugar and whisk together
4. add flour through a sieve and whisk together
5. heat milk to ~80C in a pot and add it in 2 batches to the egg yolk while whisking
6. add the egg yolk mixture back into the pot
7. continously whisk over low to medium heat until it thickens
8. add the butter and whisk it in
9. add it to the cream cheese and whisk it together
10. add egg whites to another bowl and whisk them using a handmixer until they froth up
11. then add the sugar, 1/3 at a time and continue whisking
12. whisk until stiff peaks form
13. add one scoop of the meringue to the cream cheese and whisk it in
14. fold the rest in
15. add the batter into a 15cm diameter round cake pan
16. put this cake pan into a bigger pan and add hot water around it, aiming for about 2cm of height
17. bake at 130C for 50 minutes
