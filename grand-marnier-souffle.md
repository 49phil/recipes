# Ingredients

* 1tbsp (14g) butter
* 5tbsp (63g) white sugar
  * 1tbsp (13g) for the ramekins
  * 4tbsp (50g) for the batter
* 5tsp (25g) butter
* 5tsp (16g) all-purpose flour
* 1/4cup milk
* 1tbsp brandy based orange liqueur
* 1tsp freshly grated orange zest
* 2 large eggs, separated
* 1/8tsp vanilla extract

# Recipe

1. preheat the oven to 400F (200C)
2. melt 1tbsp of butter and brush the insides of two 8oz ramekins
3. sprinkle the ramekins with 1tbsp of sugar and remove the excess
4. melt 5tsp of butter in a saucepan over medium-low heat
5. stir in flour until golden brown
6. add milk and stir continuously until smooth and thick, around 3 to 4 minutes
7. pour into a mixing bowl
    1. add orange liqueur
    2. add orange zest
    3. add egg yolk
    4. add vanilla
8. mix until smooth
9. whisk egg white in a separate bowl until frothy
10. add 2tbsp of sugar and whisk until incorporated
11. add remaning 2tbsp of sugar until it holds its shape without forming stiff peaks
12. fold the meringue into the yolk mixture one half at a time
13. transfer into prepared ramekins, leaving 1/4in (0.6cm) of space at the top
14. bake for about 16 minutes in the oven
