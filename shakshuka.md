# Ingredients

* 2tbsp olive oil
* 1 large onion
* salt
* 1 cup diced red bell pepper
* 1 jalapeno pepper
* 1tsp cumin
* 1/2tsp paprika
* 1/2tsp ground turmeric
* 1/2tsp ground black pepper
* 1/4tsp cayenne pepper
* 500g can crushed san marzano tomatoes
* water
* 3 large eggs
* 2tbsp crumbled feta cheese
* 2tbsp fresh parsley

# Recipe

1. dice the onion and red bell pepper
2. cut the jalapeno pepper
3. add olive oil to a pan over medium high heat
4. add onion and cook them until they turn translucent
5. add bell pepper and jalapeno and cook for about 5 minutes
6. add spices and stir them in for 1 to 2 minutes
    1. cumin
    2. paprika
    3. ground turmeric
    4. ground black pepper
    5. cayenne
7. add crushed tomatoes and fill the can with water, adding the same amount
9. let it simmer for 15 to 20 minutes or until the sauce is thick enough to allow for depressions for the egg
9. taste for seasoning
10. make depressions in the sauce for each egg and put heat to medium
11. crack the egg in a different container first so that the yolk doesn't break
12. season the eggs with salt and black pepper
13. put on a lid and let the eggs cook
14. once the eggs are cooked to your liking, take it off the stove
15. garnish with crumbs of feta cheese and parsley
16. serve with toasted bread
