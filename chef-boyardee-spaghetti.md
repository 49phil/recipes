# Ingredients

* 1kg canned peeled tomatoes
* 6tbsp olive oil
* 140g diced onion
* 1 1/2tsp salt
* 70g diced carrot
* 500g ground beef
* 1tsp ground pepper
* 5 medium sized basil leaves
* 40g sliced mushrooms
* 225g spaghetti
* 28g butter
* 20g grated parmesan or more if you include garnish

# Recipe

1. bolognese
    1. chop up the tomatoes and press them through a sieve
    2. discard the seeds and set the tomato sauce aside
    3. add olive oil to a sauce pan over medium heat
    4. add onion and cook them for about 3 minutes
    5. season with 3/4tsp salt
    6. add carrots and cook them for about 5 to 6 minutes or until the onions are translucent
    7. add meat and season with the rest of the salt + ground pepper, cook until the meat is no longer pink and break it up
    8. stir in tomato sauce and mushrooms
    9. bring the sauce to a boil, then reduce it to simmer
    10. place a lid on the pan leaving a slight opening and let it cook for 40 minutes
    11. stir in sliced basil and cook for another 5 minutes with the lid on
2. spaghetti
    1. cook them according to instructions
    2. drain them
    3. return them to the pot
    4. add butter and parmesan and combine
3. plate the spaghetti and add sauce on top
4. garnish with parmesan
