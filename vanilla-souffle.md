# Ingredients

* 2 eggs
* 40g white sugar
  * 10g for egg yolk
  * 30g for egg white
* 15g cake flour
* 15g butter
* 135g milk
* 1tsp vanilla extract
17. optionally: [vanilla sauce](vanilla-sauce.md)

# Recipe

1. prepare two ramekins
    1. butter the inside
    2. coat the inside with sugar
2. whisk together 2 egg yolks and 10g of sugar
3. whisk in 15g of flour
4. add 135g of room temperature milk and whisk it in
5. pour into a sauce pan over medium heat
6. continuously whisk until it thickens
7. turn off the heat and continue whisking for roughly 30 more seconds
8. add 15g of butter and whisk it in
9. add vanilla extract
10. pour into a bowl and cover with plastic wrap until needed
11. make the meringue by whisking 2 egg whites
12. once they turn frothy, slowly add the sugar and keep whisking until it will hold its shape
13. get the bowl from earlier and quickly whisk it with the hand mixer you used for the meringue
14. then fold in the meringue, one half at a time
15. pour into ramekins, flattening the top and leaving a slight edge
16. bake at 200C for 13 to 14 minutes
17. optionally: make an incision and add [vanilla sauce](vanilla-sauce.md)
