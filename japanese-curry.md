# Ingredients

* 600g vegetables cut into bite-sized chunks
  * carrots
  * potatoes
  * onions
* 2 cloves of garlic
* 1tbsp vegetable oil
* 540ml chicken stock or water
* optional:
  * 1tbsp ketchup
  * 1tbsp worcestershire sauce
  * 1tbsp honey
  * 1 sweet apple (e.g. fuji) finely grated
* 92g japanese curry mix

# Recipe

1. add vegetable oil and onions into a pan and cook until translucent
    * you can also caramelize the onions for a deeper flavor
2. add crushed garlic
3. add the rest of the vegetables and stir for a minute
4. add chicken stock or water and make sure it covers all the ingredients
5. if you want, you can now add the optional ingredients and stir them in
6. let it simmer for 15 to 20 minutes or until vegetables are tender
7. add curry mix and stir it in
8. serve with rice and chicken katsu
