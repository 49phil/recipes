# Ingredients

* 225g spinach
* 1 clove of garlic
* 1 green onion
* 1 1/2tsp soy sauce
* 1 1/2tsp sesame oil
* 2tsp toasted sesame seeds

# Recipe

1. trim off roots from spinach and wash any dirt off
2. blanch the spinach for 30 to 60 seconds with lid off in salted boiling water
3. shock spinach in cold water
4. squeeze the water out firmly
5. cut into bite sized pieces
6. chop green onion
7. mince garlic
8. add to a bowl
  1. spinach
  2. garlic
  3. green onion
  4. soy sauce
  5. toasted sesame oil
  6. sesame seeds
9. mix together
