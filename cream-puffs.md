# Ingredients

* 95g flour
  * 75g for puffs
  * 20g for custard cream
* 130ml water
* pinch of salt
* 50g butter
* 4 eggs
  * 2 for puffs
  * 2 for custard cream
* 200ml milk
* 55g sugar
  * 40g for custard cream
  * 15g for whipped cream
* 150ml whipped cream

# Recipe

1. puffs
    1. add 2 eggs to a bowl and whisk together
    2. sift flour for later
    3. add 130ml of water, 50g of butter and a pinch of salt to a pot
    4. melt over medium heat
    5. take off the heat once it starts boiling
    6. stir in the flour
    7. mix until it is fully combined
    8. put the pan back on the stove and cook over medium heat
    9. once it thickened, transfer dough to a bowl and spread it out
    10. pour eggs from earlier into the batter one half at a time
    11. whisk together until combined
    12. pipe 2 to 3cm wide circles on a baking sheet
    13. bake at 200C for 10 minutes
    14. then lower the temperature to 180C and bake for another 20 minutes
    15. lastly lower to 160C and bake for 10 more minutes
2. custard cream
    1. separate egg yolk from whites
    2. add 40g of sugar to the egg yolk and combine
    3. add 20g of flour and mix until smooth
    4. add 200ml of warm milk
    5. cook in a sauce pan over medium heat until it thickens
    6. transfer to a plate, cover and let it cool to room temperature
3. whipped cream
    1. add 150ml whipped cream to a bowl
    2. add 15g sugar
    3. whisk until it forms peaks
    4. add custard cream and whisk together
    5. add cream to a piping bag and fill the puffs
