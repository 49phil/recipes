# Ingredients

* 500g ground beef
* 1 onion
* 3/4tsp salt
* 1tsp pepper
* thymian
* 8 slices american or cheddar cheese
* 1 cup (~260g) ketchup
* 3tbsp cider vinegar
* 2tbsp brown sugar
* 2tbsp worcestershire sauce
* 3/4tsp dried thyme
* 3/4tsp dry mustard
* 3/4tsp granulated garlic
* 3/4tsp chili powder
* 1 1/4tsp pepper
  * 1tsp for meat
  * 1/4tsp for sauce
* 4 italian sub rolls

# Recipe

1. slice the onion thin
2. cook meat
    1. add oil to a pan over medium high heat
    2. add beef
    3. add onion
    4. add pepper
    5. add salt
3. break the meat apart
4. cook for about 10 minutes or until the liquid has evaporated
5. preheat oven to 175C
6. cook sauce (in the same pan as the meat)
    1. add ketchup
    2. add cider vinegar
    3. add brown sugar
    4. add worcestershire sauce
    5. add dried thyme
    6. add dry mustard
    7. add garlic powder
    8. add chili powder
    9. add pepper
7. cook until the sauce has thickened
8. slice the italian sub rolls lenght wise
9. add them on a baking sheet
10. spread meat mixture on top of the rolls
11. add 2 slices of american cheese
12. put in the oven to the middle position
13. bake until cheese has melted, around 5 minutes
