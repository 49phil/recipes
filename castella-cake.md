# Ingredients

* 5 eggs
* 55g butter
* 25g cooking oil
* 80g milk
* 2g salt
* 80g sugar
* 80g soft flour
* 1tsp vanilla extract

# Recipe

1. separate egg whites and yolks
2. leave egg whites in refrigerator until needed
3. add to a pot
    1. 80g milk
    2. 25g cooking oil
    3. 55g butter
4. over low heat, wait until 90% of the butter has melted
5. take off the heat and whisk together
6. add 2g of salt
7. add 1tsp vanilla extract
8. add flour by sifting it through a sieve
9. whisk together
10. whisk in egg yolks
11. take egg whites and whisk with a handmixer until frothy
12. add 1/3 of sugar at a time and continue mixing
13. mix until soft peaks form
14. add 2 spatulas of meringue at a time into the egg yolk mixture and mix it in for a total of 4
15. then add the meringue into the egg whites and mix from bottom to top
16. once it is incorporated, pour into a 16cm x 16cm container lined with baking sheet
17. tap it a few times on the counter to remove any bubbles
18. add into bigger pan and pour hot water around it
19. bake in the bottom compartment and bake at 150C for 55 to 60 minutes
