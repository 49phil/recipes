# Ingredients

* 75g mushrooms (use either)
  * shimeji
  * enoki
  * maitake
* 2 pieces fresh boneless salmon filets
* 2 cups rice
* 2tbsp soy sauce
* 2tbsp sake
* 2tbsp mirin
* 1tsp dashi powder
* rice cooker
* 1tbsp butter

# Recipe

1. wash rice
2. add soy sauce
3. add mirin
4. add sake
5. add dashi powder
6. add water
7. let it marinate for 30 minutes and prepare mushrooms/salmon in the meantime
8. remove skin from salmon if present
9. cut off base from shimeji mushrooms
10. if you have 150g of mushrooms, cut them in half and freeze it
11. separate the leftover mushrooms
12. after the rice has been resting for 30 minutes, add salmon and mushrooms
13. then place it in the rice cooker and cook it per the rice cooker's instructions
14. after it is done, add 1tbsp of butter as a final touch and fold it in
15. one serving size is about 150g, you can freeze the rest for later
