# Ingredients

* 200g hazelnuts
* pinch of salt
* 50g powdered sugar
* 1tsp vanilla extract
* optional: 3tbsp neutral oil to smoothen the texture

# Recipe

1. remove outer skin (skip if you have blanched hazelnuts)
    1. roast the hazelnuts at 175C for 8 to 10 minutes
    2. let them cool down for around 20 to 30 minutes
    3. lay out a kitchen towel
    4. spread the hazel nuts on top, then wrap them in the towel and rub the skin off
2. add them to your food processor
3. add a pinch of salt
4. add powdered sugar
5. add vanilla extract
6. optional: add neutral oil to smoothen the texture
7. blend until it reached your desired level of smoothness
