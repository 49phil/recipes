# Ingredients

* soy sauce
* mirin
* sugar
* spam
* short-grain white rice

# Recipe

1. start making the rice and let it cool before assembling
2. combine 1 part soy sauce, mirin and sugar and set aside
3. cut spam into 3/4cm thick slices
3. brown the spam in a pan until it develops a nice color
4. add soy sauce mixture
5. once the soy sauce has thickened and the spam took on a nice deep color, prepare the musubi
6. assemble (easiest using a form, otherwise you can also use the can the spam came in)
    1. lay down a nori leaf
    2. add spam
    3. add rice
    4. fold the nori over
