# Ingredients

* ~750ml tomato puree
* ~750ml water
* olive oil
  * 1tbsp for tomato broth
  * 1/4cup for spaghetti
* 2 cloves of garlic
* salt
* 2tsp red chili flakes
* 170g spaghetti
* 1tbsp finely chopped parsley for garnish

# Recipe

1. tomato broth
    1. add 1tbsp of olive oil to a sauce pan over medium heat
    2. add 2 finely chopped cloves of garlic and let it sizzle for around 60 seconds
    3. add tomato puree
    4. add water
    5. add salt
2. spaghetti
    1. add 1/4 cup of olive oil to a pan (make sure the pan is wide enough for the spaghetti) over medium-high heat
    2. add chili flakes
    3. once the chili flakes sizzled for around a minute, add spaghetti
    4. toss the spaghetti in the oil to coat all noodles
    5. add around for 4 ladles of tomato broth to cover the spaghetti
    6. mix the spaghetti with the broth but keep them all in the same direction
    7. once the tomato broth has been mostly absorbed/evaporated, turn the spaghetti so that the top side can develop a crust
    8. keep cooking until the bottom has developed a nice crust
    9. add more tomato broth, less than the first time
    10. mix the spaghetti with the broth, you can curl the spaghetti now
    11. let the broth evaporate again and develop a crust on the bottom again
    12. repeat until pasta is cooked to your liking (for al dente, this will probably take 20 to 25 minutes)
    13. add salt to taste
3. garnish with parsley
