# Ingredients

* 70g bitter chocolate (67%)
* 3 eggs
* 50g milk
* 80g sugar
* 20g flour
* 20g cocoa powder
* 35g butter

# Recipe

1. sift the flour and cocoa powder and whisk it together
2. separate egg yolk and whites
3. add sugar to the egg white
4. whisk until soft and shiny
5. add chocolate, milk and butter in a bowl over a double boiler
6. heat until the ingredients melted together
7. take off the boiler and wait half a minute
8. add the egg yolk and whisk them in
9. add half of the meringue and fold it in
10. add the flour and cocoa powder from earlier and whisk it in
11. add the rest of the meringue and fold it in
12. add batter to a 15cm round mold
13. put the mould in a bigger form and add around 1/2 to 1cm of hot water around it
14. bake at 160C for 60 minutes
