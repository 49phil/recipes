# Ingredients

* 2 eggs
* 35g sugar
* 160g milk
* 1tsp vanilla extract

# Recipe

1. whisk together 2 eggs yolks and sugar
2. heat milk to 50C in a sauce pan
3. whisk the milk into the egg yolk
4. pour into sauce pan and heat until it reaches 80C while whisking
5. when it reaches 80C, turn off the heat and whisk until it reaches 82C
6. sift the sauce through a sieve
7. add 1tsp of vanilla extract
