# Ingredients

* 80ml water
* 440ml milk
  * 80ml for choux pastry
  * 360ml for chocolate cream filling
* 180g butter
  * 70g for choux pastry
  * 110g for chocolate cream filling
* pinch of salt
* 115g flour
* vanilla
* 70g sugar
* 30g corn starch
* 6 eggs
  * 3 for choux pastry
  * 3 for chocolate cream filling
* 100g chocolate

# Recipe

1. choux pastry
    1. add 80ml water and 80ml milk to a sauce pan
    2. add 70g of butter
    3. add pinch of salt
    4. cook over medium heat until the butter melted
    5. once it is boiling, lower the heat and add 115g of flour
    6. stir it in and let the dough dry for 3 to 5 minutes
    7. move dough to a bowl and spread it out to cool
    8. add 3 (or 4 depending on consistency) eggs to the dough, one at a time
        1. if a 4th egg is needed, whisk it before and add one half at a time to ensure the dough isn't too runny
    9. dough should be soft and pliable
    10. pipe dough onto a baking sheet
    11. wet your fingers with water and smooth out the tips
    12. bake at 190C for 15 minutes, then reduce to 170C and bake for another 25-30 minutes (check closely in the last 5 minutes)
2. chocolate cream filling
    1. separate 3 eggs
    2. add 70g of sugar to the egg yolks
    3. add 30g of corn starch to the egg yolks
    4. whisk together until smooth
    5. add 360ml milk to a sauce pan
    6. heat milk over low until it simmers
    7. add a bit of the hot milk to the egg yolks and immediately whisk it in
    8. add the rest of the heated milk in batches until all is incorporated
    9. add it back into the sauce pan
    10. over medium heat, continously whisk until it thickens
    11. take off the heat, add 55g of butter to the custard and whisk it in
    12. add 100g of chocolate to the custard and whisk it in
    13. move it into a chilled container to stop it from cooking further
    14. add 55g of soft butter to a bowl and whisk it for a minute
    15. add the custard and whisk it in
3. pipe the custard into the choux pastry from earlier
