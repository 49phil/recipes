# Ingredients

* 1 vanilla bean (alternatively, 1tsp ground vanilla)
* 1/2cup white sugar
* 5tbsp cornstarch
* 1/2tsp kosher salt
* 5 large eggs
  * 3 for the pastry cream
  * 2 for the pâte à choux
* 2cups whole milk
* 8tbsp unsalted butter
  * 4tbsp (57g) for pastry cream
  * 4tbsp (57g) for pâte à choux
* 1/2cup heavy cream
* 3/4cup (200g) dark chocolate chips
* garnish
  * cocoa powder
  * raspberries

# Recipe

1. add to a sauce pan for the pastry cream
    1. add vanilla seeds/ground vanilla
    2. add sugar
    3. add salt
    4. add 2 egg yolks and one egg
    5. add milk
2. whisk until combined
3. place over medium heat and cook, stirring constantly
4. whisk until thick, about 10 minutes, be prepared as this can happen quite abruptly depending on your stove
5. remove from the heat and whisk a bit more to release some steam
6. add butter and whisk it in
7. pass through a sieve and refrigerate for at least 3 hours
8. preheat oven to 425F (215C)
9. line a baking sheet with parchment paper
10. add to a sauce pan for the choux pastry
    1. add butter
    2. add 1/2cup water
    3. add salt
11. bring to a simmer over medium-high heat
12. add flour and stir until a buttery dough forms
13. continue stirring until a starchy film forms on the bottom
14. turn off the heat and stir for another minute
15. transfer to a bowl and stir in one egg at a time
16. transfer into a pastry bag
17. pipe your desired shape on the baking sheet (to fix any imperfections, dip your fingers in water)
18. bake in the center of the oven for around 30 minutes
19. after baking, let it cool in the oven with the door slightly open
20. after the choux pastry cooled, slice it in half
21. pipe the refrigerated pastry cream onto the bottom half of the choux pastry and place top half back on top
22. add chocolate chips to a bowl
23. heat heavy cream in a sauce pan
23. once it simmers, pour the cream over the chocolate chips
24. let it sit for 2 to 3 minutes
25. stir until the ganache is thick and shiny
26. let it cool for about 5 minutes, otherwise the ganache is too runny
27. spoon ganache on top of the choux pastry
28. place eclair on serving plate, optionally sift cocoa powder over top and add raspberries as garnish
