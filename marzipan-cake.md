# Ingredients

* 200g marzipan
* 200g flour
* 2tsp baking powder
* 175g sugar
* 8g vanilla sugar
* salt
* 8 drops bitter almond flavor
* 4 eggs
* 200g butter
* 75g dark chocolate chips
* 125g dark chocolate glaze

# Recipe

1. butter the cake pan and add flour to the sides
2. preheat the oven to 170C
3. add to a mixing bowl
    1. marzipan cut into smaller chunks
    2. flour
    3. baking powder
    4. sugar
    5. vanilla sugar
    6. pinch of salt
    7. almond flavor
    8. eggs
    9. butter
4. whisk together, slowly at first, then on the highest setting of your hand mixer
5. mix until the batter is smooth
6. add batter to the cake pan
7. bake for about 70 minutes
8. let it cool for 10 minutes
9. take the cake out of the pan
10. add water to a pot and cook until it simmers
11. take it off the stove and add a pack of chocolate glaze into it
12. take the chocolate glaze out after 10 minutes
13. knead the pack, cut off one corner and apply it to the cake
14. wait until the glaze turns solid
