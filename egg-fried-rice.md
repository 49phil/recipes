# Ingredients

* jasmine rice
* 4 eggs
* 2tbsp soy sauce
* spring onion
* vegetables (stay away from wet vegetables, otherwise use whatever you fancy)
  * green beans
  * carrots
  * sweet corn

# Recipe

1. easiest to use cold leftover rice, otherwise leave freshly made rice out for around 10 minutes to cool
2. add oil to a wok over high heat
3. add egg and stir
4. add vegetables that cook slower
5. add rice
6. add 2tbsp of soy sauce
7. add vegetables that cook fast
