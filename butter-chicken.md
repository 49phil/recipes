# Ingredients

* vegetable oil
* 2tbsp butter
* 1 medium yellow onion
* 1 1/2
* 1 1/2lbs (600g) boneless chicken thighs
* 1tbsp garam masala
* 1tsp ground cumin
* 1tsp smoked paprika
* 1/2tsp ground tumeric
* 1/8tsp ground cinnamon
* 1/8tsp cayenne pepper
* 1tbsp freshly grated/minced ginger
* 4 cloves of garlic
* 1/4cup tomato paste
* 1/2cup chicken broth
* 1cup heavy cream

# Recipe

1. dice the onion
2. grate the ginger and set aside
3. crush the garlic and set aside
4. cut the chicken into 1 1/2inch pieces
5. add 1tbsp of vegetable oil and 1tbsp of butter to a pan over medium-high heat
6. add the onion and season with salt
7. once the onions have turned translucent, add the chicken
8. add the spices
    1. garam masala
    2. cumin
    3. smoked paprika
    4. turmeric
    5. cinnamon
    6. cayenne
9. mix everything and let it sear for around 4 minutes total
10. make space in the middle of the pan and add 1tsp of vegetable oil
11. add grated ginger and crushed garlic
12. let it sizzle for 30 seconds while stirring into the chicken
13. make space in the center again and add tomato paste
14. mix the tomato paste into the chicken and cook until it starts to caramelize onto the bottom (around 2 minutes)
15. add chicken broth
16. add cream
17. stir both in and scrape everything off the bottom
18. let it come to a simmer and reduce the temperature to medium-low
19. let the sauce thicken for about 3 to 4 minutes
20. reduce heat to low and add 1tbsp of cold butter
21. serve with rice
