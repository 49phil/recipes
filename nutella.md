# Ingredients

* 200g hazelnuts
* pinch of salt
* 50g powdered sugar
* 25 to 50g unsweetened cocoa powder (depending on desired consistency)
* 1tsp vanilla extract
* optional: 1-2tbsp neutral oil to smoothen the texture

# Recipe

1. remove outer skin (skip if you have blanched hazelnuts or prefer a slightly bitter flavor)
    1. roast the hazelnuts at 175C for 8 to 10 minutes
    2. let them cool down for around 20 to 30 minutes
    3. lay out a kitchen towel
    4. spread the hazel nuts on top, then wrap them in the towel and rub the skin off
2. add them to your food processor
3. add a pinch of salt
4. blend until the oil leaks out (if you add all ingredients add once, this step will take ages)
5. add powdered sugar
6. add unsweetened cocoa powder
7. add vanilla extract
8. optional: add neutral oil to smoothen the texture
9. continue blending until it reached your desired level of smoothness
