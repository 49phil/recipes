# Ingredients

* amount for 31x21cm and 6cm high baking dish
* 28 savoiardi
* 500g mascarpone
* 6 eggs
* 80g sugar
* pinch of salt
* two café americanos
* unsweetened cocoa powder
* optional
  * some type of alcohol to add to the coffee (marsala wine, amaretto, dark rum etc.)

# Recipe

1. pull two shots café americano
    1. usually 1:4 ratio coffee to water, so one double shot (~40g) needs 160g of water, though you can also do 1:3 or 1:5 ratio
    2. therefore, pull two shots and add 360ml hot (60 to 70C) water
2. while the coffee cools to room temperature, prepare the mascarpone
    1. separate eggs
    2. add half of the sugar and a pinch of salt to the egg white
    3. whisk the egg white until stiff peaks form
    4. whisk the egg yolk with the other half of the sugar
    5. add the egg yolk to the mascarpone and whisk together
    6. then add the egg whites to the mascarpone and fold them in as to not deflate it
3. assemble
    1. dip each savoiardi into the room temperature coffee, make sure both sides are coated without them falling apart
    2. assemble a layer into your baking dish
    3. add mascarpone cream layer on top
    4. then add another layer of coffee soaked savoiardi
    5. finish with another layer of mascarpone
    6. cover with plastic wrap and pop in the fridge for 6 to 8 hours or preferably overnight
4. take out of the fridge and garnish with cocoa powder
